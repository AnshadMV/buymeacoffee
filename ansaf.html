Questions

Quick answers before you pour that coffee.

How much is one coffee?

One coffee is ₹10. Buy any whole number from 1 to 1000, so 5 coffees is ₹50.

How do I pay?

Tap Support, then scan the QR with any UPI app or tap “Open UPI app”. The receiver and amount are filled in and you confirm with your UPI PIN. Afterwards tap “I’ve paid” to join the wall.

Is this UPI AutoPay?

Not yet. True AutoPay mandates need a registered merchant or payment gateway, which a personal UPI ID can’t create. The weekly reminder is a repeating Google Calendar event that brings you back to pay in one tap.

Are payments verified here?

No. The wall entry is based on your tap of “I’ve paid”. Ansaf confirms the actual money in the UPI app.
